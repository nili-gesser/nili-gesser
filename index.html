About

Nili Gesser is a senior lecturer in the Department of Criminology at Bar-Ilan University. She received her Ph.D. from the Department of Criminal Justice at Temple University in 2021, an LL.M. from Bar-Ilan University, and an LL.B. from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.

Before joining Bar-Ilan University, she was a research fellow in the Department of Criminology and Justice Studies at Drexel University and a postdoctoral research fellow in the Anderson Sexual Violence Prevention Lab at the University of North Dakota. She is also a researcher with the Community Justice and Equity group at the University of Maryland School of Social Work.

Current Projects

Bars or Bridges

Principal investigator in a mixed-method study examining the consequences of incarceration versus community service for women committing low-level crimes in the Israeli prison system.

Protect Your Spirit

Community-based participatory research to establish an agenda of sexual assault research for Indigenous populations.

SCOPE

Research on a SEPTA program designed to divert vulnerable populations away from incarceration and increase public safety.

Pushing Progress Philly

Evaluation of an intervention to reduce gun violence and promote social justice among young men at high risk of gun violence in Philadelphia.
